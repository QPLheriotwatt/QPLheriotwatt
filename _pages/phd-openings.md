# PhD Positions

**We are currently open for PhD applications related to our ERC CoG grant. The positions are mostly related to the Electron Pair Microscopy projects.**

We are looking for new PhD students with passion, talent, and grit!

You will have the chance to work on the grand challenges of condensed matter physics, often at the interface of instrumental design and new physics. You will be involved in determining the important and interesting questions, creating and improving instrumental setups, performing measurements, and making discoveries.

### Applications for PhD positions
If you are interested in working with us as a PhD student, please send me an [email](mailto:milan.allan@gmail.com). State briefly why you are interested and attach a CV, including information about the grades you had as an undergraduate. No need for a separate cover letter or certificates. **Important**: please insert _"Application PhD"_ in the subject line. If you are applying to a specific advertisement, note this in your email.

### PhD projects for Heriot-Watt University students
If you are a Master student at Heriot-Watt University looking for a PhD project, contact me (or any group member) per email or stop by my office.

### PhD students from elsewhere
If you are interested in pursuing a PhD degree at Heriot-Watt University, see our university website for more information. Sometimes, we take PhD students if we get exceptional applicants (this usually means very good grades and a personal recommendation).
